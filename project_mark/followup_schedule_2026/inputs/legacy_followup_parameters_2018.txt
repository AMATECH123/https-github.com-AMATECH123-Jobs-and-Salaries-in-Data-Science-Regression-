Follow up parameters, Business Activity Survey, 2018 (pre COS)

Unit: questionnaire.
Reminder share: 60 per cent of questionnaires returned.
Escalation share: 95 per cent of questionnaires returned.
Rounding: boundaries rounded to the nearest 15 minutes.
Population: all questionnaires returned in the two preceding years, pooled.
Superseded by the Collection Operations Standard from 1 January 2020.

The Fall 2026 bus rating is in effect, and Finance and Service Planning disagree on how much scheduled weekday bus service changed from the Summer 2026 rating to the Fall 2026 rating. Finance says 321 more weekday trips. Service Planning says 121. The Board takes one certified figure, counted the way it always has been: scheduled trips on MBTA bus routes on a typical school day weekday, Monday through Thursday, under each rating. The attached workspace is the schedule data and reference material our team works from. Certify the net change in weekday bus trips between the two ratings and name the single route that accounts for the largest share of it.

Build the register behind the figure as a CSV, route_service_change.csv: one row per MBTA bus route scheduled under either rating, with the route, its service category, its weekday trips under each rating, the change and percent change, and the scheduled revenue time it runs that weekday under each rating with the change, then one total row with the two system counts, the certified net change, and the system revenue time. Route rows must add up exactly to the total row.

For the Board slide, make a waterfall as a PNG, service_change_bridge.png, from the Summer weekday count to the Fall weekday count with one bar per route whose count changed, biggest change first. Label every bar with its change, label both end totals and the net change, and make the driving route stand out.

Then write the Board memo as a PDF, certification_memo.pdf, two pages at most. Open with the certified net change and the driving route, then say what each office's figure actually counted and why you are not certifying it. Give the ranked table of every route whose weekday count changed, with both counts and the change. State how many trips in each rating's count are school day supplemental trips, and the change in scheduled weekday revenue time that goes with the certified trip change and whether the same route leads on it. For the driving route, give the first scheduled departure and last scheduled arrival of the weekday under each rating and describe what changed on the route. Close with the smallest change in a single route's Fall count that would make a different route the driver, and name that route.

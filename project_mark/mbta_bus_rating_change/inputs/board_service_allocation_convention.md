# Added weekday round trip: service allocation convention

Scenario document. Written by the task author to state the convention the Board applies in this task. It is not
an MBTA publication. The ridership counts and the schedule feeds it is applied to are real and unaltered.

1. What is certified. For each fall rating the Board certifies the weekday peak share of Commuter Rail boardings
   and places the rating's added weekday round trip: in the peak when the certified share has held or risen since
   the previous certification, in the off peak when it has fallen.

2. The count. Boardings are taken from the MBTA's published count of Commuter Rail ridership by trip, season,
   line and stop: the latest season for the rating, and the season of the previous certification for the
   comparison. Counts are certified as published: no count is rescaled or adjusted.

3. Boardings. A counted train is identified by its line, train number and direction as the count lists them. Its
   weekday boardings are the sum of the average boardings the count reports at its stops.

4. Periods. A train is a peak train when it is an inbound train scheduled to leave its first stop between 05:30
   and 08:29, or an outbound train scheduled to leave its first stop between 15:30 and 18:29, both inclusive.
   Every other train is an off peak train.

5. The counted train in the schedule. A counted train is the scheduled train of its season carrying the same train
   number in the same direction, train numbers being compared by their digits. A counted train that the season's
   schedule does not list cannot be placed; it is left out of the period totals and reported.

6. The share. The peak share is peak boardings as a percentage of all placed boardings, stated to one decimal
   place. The change is the difference in percentage points between the two shares, taken before either is
   rounded and stated to one decimal place.

7. Seasons and schedules. The Fall 2024 count is read against the weekday schedule of Wednesday 16 October 2024.
   The Spring 2018 count is read against the weekday schedule of Wednesday 23 May 2018.

8. The rating's weekday schedule. The weekday schedule in effect on the greatest number of Monday to Friday dates
   of the rating. The Fall 2026 rating runs from 28 September 2026 to 11 December 2026. Lines are the routes the
   feed places in the Commuter Rail fare class.

9. Previous certification. Spring 2018, certified from the Spring 2018 count on this convention; the added round
   trip was placed in the peak.

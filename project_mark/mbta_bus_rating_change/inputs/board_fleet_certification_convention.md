# Weekday bus requirement: certification convention

Scenario document. Written by the task author to state the certification convention the Board applies in this
task. It is not an MBTA publication. The schedule data it is applied to is real and unaltered.

1. What is certified. For each rating the Board certifies the weekday bus requirement: the greatest number of
   buses in service at any one minute of the service day.

2. A bus in service. A bus is in service from the scheduled departure of the first trip of its day's work to
   the scheduled arrival of the last trip of that work. Time between trips is in service. A trip is not a bus.

3. The certification day. The requirement is measured on a Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday that runs the
   weekday schedule in effect for the greatest number of weekdays in the rating, with no holiday, modified, or
   reduced schedule in force. Fridays are not used.

4. The population. Every route the MBTA classifies as a bus route in its route category field. Rail replacement
   shuttles are not bus routes. Trips on bus routes are counted once each, under the route that owns the trip.

5. Comparison. The change certified for a rating is measured against the figure certified for the previous
   rating on the same convention.

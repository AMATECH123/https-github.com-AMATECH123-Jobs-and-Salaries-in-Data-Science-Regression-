# Statistical Compendium, attitudes chapter: series standard

Scenario document. Written by the task author to state the standard the compendium's attitudes chapter applies
to the long run series it carries in this task. It is not a NORC publication. The survey data it is applied to
is the General Social Survey cumulative data file, real and unaltered.

1. Source. A series is built from the General Social Survey cumulative data file as supplied in the workspace,
   with variables, codes, years, ballots and versions as the codebook defines them.

2. Indicator. A figure is the share of respondents who gave the counted response among the respondents who
   gave any of the question's listed responses in that survey year. Respondents without a listed response, for
   whatever reason, are outside the denominator.

3. Instrument and sample. A figure for a survey year is an estimate for the adult population from the full
   sample of that year, that is from every respondent the question was fielded to, on the standard instrument:
   the question wording and the set of response options offered to the respondent as they stood in the base
   year. A figure computed from respondents selected by interview mode, from respondents assigned to an
   experimental version of the question, or from a version that offered a different set of response options,
   is not a figure for the series.

4. Weight. Every figure is weighted by the person post stratification weight, WTSSPS.

5. Sampling error. The sampling error of a figure, and of a change between two years, is estimated under the
   survey design from the variance strata and primary sampling units carried in the file, each survey year an
   independent sample. Years for which the file carries no design variables show no sampling error.

6. Years. The series carries every survey year in which the question was fielded. The edition year figure and
   the decade change (the edition year figure less the base year figure, base year 2014 for the 2026 edition)
   are published only when the edition year has a figure under rule 3. When it does not, the series carries the
   latest survey year with a figure under rule 3, labelled with its year, and publishes no change.

7. Continuity. Before a series is carried into a new edition, the figures the previous edition printed are
   reproduced from the current file under that edition's method. A printed figure that reproduces but does not
   meet rule 3 is withdrawn in the new edition, and the edition says so.

8. Reporting and precision. A change is reported as significant when it is at least 1.96 times its sampling
   error. Figures are published to one decimal place in percent, sampling errors to two decimal places, and
   respondent counts unweighted.

# Trust series, 2024 edition: method note

Scenario document. Written by the task author to record the method on which the 2024 edition printed the trust
series (trust_series_2024_edition.csv). The figures in that table were computed by the task author from the
cumulative file in the workspace by this method; they stand in for the figures the edition printed.

- Data: the General Social Survey cumulative data file, every survey year from 1972 to 2022 in which the
  question "Generally speaking, would you say that most people can be trusted or that you can't be too careful
  in dealing with people?" was fielded. The 2021 survey is shown as not fielded.
- Figure: the share answering "most people can be trusted" among respondents giving any listed response.
- Weight: WTSSPS. Sampling error under the survey design from the variance strata and primary sampling units,
  shown where the file carries them.
- Figures to one decimal place in percent, sampling errors to two decimal places, respondent counts unweighted.

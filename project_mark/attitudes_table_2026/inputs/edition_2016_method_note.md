# Attitudes table, 2016 edition: method note

Scenario document. Written by the task author to record the method on which the 2016 edition's table
(attitudes_table_2016_edition.csv) was built. The figures in that table were computed by the task author from
the cumulative file in the workspace by this method; they stand in for the figures the edition printed.

- Data: the General Social Survey cumulative data file, 1987 and 2014 surveys.
- Indicators: each is the share of respondents giving the counted response among respondents giving any of the
  question's listed responses. Respondent counts are unweighted counts of the respondents in the denominator.
- Weight: WTSSALL, the cumulative person weight current at the time of the edition. For the 1987 survey the
  Black oversample weight OVERSAMP is applied in addition, since WTSSALL does not adjust for the 1982 and 1987
  oversamples.
- Figures to one decimal place in percent.

# Attitudes chapter: series standard

1. A series is built from the General Social Survey cumulative data file as supplied.
2. A figure is the share of respondents giving the counted response among the respondents who gave any of the
   question's listed responses in that survey year.
3. A figure for a survey year is an estimate for the adult population from the full sample of that year, on the
   standard instrument.
4. Figures are weighted with WTSSPS. Sampling error is estimated under the survey design, from VSTRAT and VPSU,
   with each survey year an independent sample; years without design variables show no sampling error.
5. The series carries every survey year in which the question was fielded. The edition year figure and the
   decade change (edition year less base year, base year 2014 for the 2026 edition) are published only when the
   edition year has a figure under rule 3. Otherwise the series carries the latest survey year that has one,
   labelled with its year, and publishes no change.
6. Before a series is carried into a new edition, the figures the previous edition printed are reproduced under
   its method. A printed figure that does not meet rule 3 is withdrawn, and the edition says so.
7. A change is significant at 1.96 times its sampling error. Figures to one decimal place in percent, sampling
   errors to two, respondent counts unweighted.

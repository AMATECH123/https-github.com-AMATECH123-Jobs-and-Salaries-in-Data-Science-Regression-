# Trust series, 2024 edition: method note

Figures in trust_series_2024_edition.csv: share answering that most people can be trusted, among respondents
giving any listed response, for every survey year 1972 to 2022 in which the question was fielded; 2021 shown as
not fielded. Weight WTSSPS. Sampling error under the survey design from VSTRAT and VPSU where the file carries
them. Figures to one decimal place, errors to two, respondent counts unweighted.

# Midwest claims note: first half of 2026

Scenario document. Written by the task author as the office's mid year note, exactly as it was published. It is
the statement under review. Not a Department of Labor or Opportunity Insights publication; the record its
figures were computed from is the real, unaltered claims record in this workspace.

Regional research office, publications desk. Published 10 July 2026.

Regular program initial unemployment insurance claims across the twelve states of the Midwest Region totalled
1,058,808 in the first half of 2026, against 1,211,608 in the first half of 2025, a fall of 12.6 percent.

Basis as labelled: U.S. Department of Labor weekly state claims, regular program initial claims, counts, summed
over Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, Ohio, South
Dakota and Wisconsin. First half of 2026: the weeks ending Saturday 3 January to Saturday 27 June 2026. First
half of 2025: the weeks ending Saturday 4 January to Saturday 28 June 2025. Change stated to one decimal place.

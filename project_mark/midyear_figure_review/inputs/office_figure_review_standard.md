# Published figures: review standard

Scenario document. Written by the task author to state the standard the office applies when a published figure
is questioned. It is not a Department of Labor or Opportunity Insights publication. The claims record it is
applied to is real and unaltered.

1. Scope. This standard governs the review of any figure the office has published on initial unemployment
   insurance claims. The record for the review is the state weekly claims file in the workspace, regular
   program initial claims as counts, weeks identified by the Saturday they end on, as the file dates them. The
   Midwest Region is the Census Bureau's: Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri,
   Nebraska, North Dakota, Ohio, South Dakota and Wisconsin.

2. The statement. A published figure is reviewed as the whole statement it was published in: every number the
   statement carries, and the basis its label gives. A basis is a complete specification of a computation: the
   states included, the weeks included in each period compared, the series column, and the formula.

3. Reproduction. A basis reproduces a statement when every number in the statement equals the number computed
   from the record on that basis, at the precision published: counts to the claim, percentages to the decimal
   place published. A basis that matches some of the numbers and not others does not reproduce the statement.

4. The review proceeds in order and stops at the first outcome reached.
   (a) The statement is computed on the basis its label gives. If that basis reproduces it, the outcome is
       that the figure stands, and the person who questioned it is sent the reproduction.
   (b) Otherwise the reviewer establishes the basis the figure was computed on, by testing alternative
       specifications until one reproduces every number in the statement. If one is established, the outcome
       is that the figure is reissued: published again with its true basis in its label, with the figure on the
       basis of the original label published beside it, and the difference between the two accounted for from
       the record.
   (c) If no basis reproduces the statement, the outcome is that the figure is withdrawn: a correction notice
       publishes the figure on the basis of the original label in its place.

5. What establishes a basis is reproduction from the record and nothing else. An explanation that is not
   demonstrated by reproduction, such as a revision to the record since publication, a clerical error, or
   rounding, does not establish a basis and does not move a review from one outcome to another.

6. Formula and precision. A percentage change is one hundred times the later period's total divided by the
   earlier period's total, less one, stated to the decimal place published, rounded half up. The office's half
   year is the weeks ending on Saturdays from 1 January to 30 June of the year.

7. Outcomes are stated in the words of this standard: stands, reissued, withdrawn.
